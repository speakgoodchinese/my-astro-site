## Source Text

> 天地有大美而不言，四时有明法而不议，万物有成理而不说。

Nature possess great beauty, yet they do not speak. The seasons possess a clear regularity, yet they do not debate. All things possess an inherent principle, yet they offer no explanations.

The true Dao is not based on textual artifacts, whether scripture, statute, code, or textbook. It is not an administrative blueprint meant to be mechanically conformed to by the masses. To seek the Dao in printed doctrines or institutional dogmas is to mistake the finger pointing at the moon for the moon itself.

### The Innate Order vs. Human Artifacts

Every individual is meant to ground their Dao in what exists within nature, following the pristine rhythm of their own innate being.

Debating the veracuity of the Dao is an exercise in [[天下神器|futility]]. The reality of the Dao is to be directly comprehended by the individual through observation of the world as it is, not through the clever rhetoric concocted by logicians whose abstractions bear no resemblance to raw reality. Big words and grand systems usually signify a profound detachment from true understanding.

### Beyond Science and Discourse

Even empirical science and rigorous experiments, at best, explain what can be observed with our mind, but not the nature of the mind itself. Laboratories and academies are just more variants of the human theater for fear and greed.

True alignment requires no defense. It is silent, self-evident, and untouched by human argument.
