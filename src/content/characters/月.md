月 is the moon and, by its cyclical phases, the month. The moon is the pre-eminent lyrical image in Chinese verse — no poet is more associated with it than 李白 Li Bai, who raised his cup to invite the moon (举杯邀明月), drowned (legend has it) trying to embrace its reflection in the Yangtze. The paired solar-lunar binary 日月 (sun-moon) stands for brightness, clarity, and, as 光明, enlightenment itself.
