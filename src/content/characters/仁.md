仁 is the highest virtue in the Confucian tradition. Its graphic composition — 人 (person) next to 二 (two) — hints at its meaning: how one person relates to another. The 论语 Analects record Confucius saying the practice of 仁 consists, in everyday terms, of 克己复礼 overcoming the self and returning to ritual propriety, and, more simply still, 爱人 loving others.
