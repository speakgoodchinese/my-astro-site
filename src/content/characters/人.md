人 is the simplest pictogram of a standing figure, two strokes representing the legs. A great deal of classical Chinese thought is organised around what a 人 can become: 君子 the noble person, 小人 the petty person, 圣人 the sage. The 中庸 Doctrine of the Mean states: "To be sincere (诚) is the Way of Heaven; to become sincere is the Way of Man" (诚者天之道也，诚之者人之道也).
