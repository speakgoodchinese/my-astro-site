国 is the state or nation. Its traditional form 國 graphically contains 囗 a border, 戈 a spear (defensive force), and 口 mouths — population under arms within a boundary. The simplified 国 replaces the inner detail with 玉 (yù jade), symbolising the preciousness of the realm. 天下国家 "all-under-heaven, the state, and the family" is the classic descending scale of moral-political concern.
