心 represents both the physical heart and the psycho-moral centre of the person — what in Western languages is split between "heart" and "mind". A great deal of Neo-Confucian 宋明理学 speculation turned on 心: whether it is innately good (孟子), whether it must be rectified (正心 the "rectifying the mind" step of the 大学), and how it relates to one's inherent nature (性 xìng).
