水 water is one of the most potent images in the Daoist canon. 道德经 The Daodejing says: "The highest goodness is like water. Water benefits the ten thousand things and does not contend; it settles in the places people disdain, and therefore is near to the Way" (上善若水). It succeeds because it yields, wears down stone not through force but through persistence, and flows always downward — the image of humility.
