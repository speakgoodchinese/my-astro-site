中 is the middle, the centre, and the state of "hitting the mark" exactly — neither overshooting nor falling short. The 中庸 Zhongyong, or Doctrine of the Mean, takes this character as the title of the fourth of the Four Books: 中 is the proper measure of every emotion before it is expressed; 和 harmony is their proper measure once expressed. 中 is also the first character in 中国 Zhōngguó China.
