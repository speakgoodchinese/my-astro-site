山 is the pictogram of three peaks rising from the ground. It evokes endurance, stability, and moral loftiness. Confucius said: "The wise delight in rivers; the humane delight in mountains" (知者乐水，仁者乐山). Mountains are the home of Daoist hermits and temples, and appear frequently in classical landscape (山水 shānshuǐ) painting.
