语 is speech, language, or a saying. Its left radical 讠(言) ties it to the semantic field of words; the right side 吾 (wō, I) gives the sound, and, some would say, a hint of meaning — the language of the self. 汉语 Hànyǔ is the Chinese language; 华语 Huáyǔ is another common name for it in overseas communities.
