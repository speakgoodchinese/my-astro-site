风 wind is an image of invisible, pervasive influence. By extension it means style, ethos, or public mood: 风气 public ethos, 风度 personal bearing. The 诗经 Book of Songs divides into 风 Airs (popular songs), 雅 Elegantiae, 颂 Hymns — the word 风 names the folk-stratum of poetry that carried the feel (the "wind") of the common people up to the ruler's ear.
