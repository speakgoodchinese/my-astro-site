学 means to learn, to study, and by extension a scholarly tradition or school. The 大学 Great Learning opens with the three guiding clauses of higher learning: manifesting luminous virtue, renewing the people, and resting in the highest good. Together with 习 (xí, to practice and review), it forms 学习 (xuéxí), the modern word for study — learning is never complete without repeated practice.
