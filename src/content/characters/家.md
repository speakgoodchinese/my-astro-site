家 is family and home. Its etymological picture — a roof 宀 over a pig 豕 — reflects the ancient household with livestock. Confucian ethics organises much of its moral reasoning around the five relationships (五伦), of which the family (父父子子 夫夫妇妇) is the foundation. 家 also forms compounds for schools of thought: 儒家 Rújiā Confucianism, 道家 Dàojiā Daoism.
