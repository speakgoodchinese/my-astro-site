德 is virtue or excellence, understood not as a mere list of rules obeyed externally, but as an inwardly cultivated disposition that radiates outward. The compound 道德 (dàodé) captures the unity of the objective Way (道) and its subjective embodiment in a person (德). A person of 德 has mastered both outer conduct and inner equanimity.
