道 is one of the foundational concepts of Chinese philosophy. It denotes both a literal path or road, and, by extension, the way in which all things naturally unfold — the unforced, immanent pattern of reality. In 道家 Daoist thought, 道 is the non-intentional, non-anthropomorphic ground of being; in 儒家 Confucian discourse, it refers to the moral Way of the ancients and the cultivated path of the noble person (君子).
