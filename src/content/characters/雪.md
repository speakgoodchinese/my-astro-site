Its radical 雨 yǔ (rain) sits atop a phonetic 彐. 风花雪月 (fēng huā xuě yuè — wind, flowers, snow, moon) is a classical phrase naming the four stock images of lyrical poetry, and, by extension, romantic or aestheticist literary style. "独钓寒江雪" — "fishing alone, in the cold river snow" from 柳宗元's quintessential quatrain 江雪 River Snow is one of the most famous lines in all Chinese verse.
