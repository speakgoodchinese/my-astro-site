天 is the great above — the sky, the heavens, and, in classical discourse, the all-encompassing moral-natural order that oversees the human realm but does not act as a personal deity. The compound 天地 (tiāndì heaven-and-earth) denotes the cosmos as a whole, the context in which 人 must find their place. The 易经 speaks of 自强不息 ceaseless self-strengthening, following the image of 天's unending movement.
