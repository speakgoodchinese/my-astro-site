小 is small. Ethically, 小人 the petty or small person stands in perennial contrast to 君子 the noble person in Confucian discourse. Yet the Daoist tradition also praises the small: 道德经 notes that the great Way fills the smallest corners, and the soft, the weak, and the tiny (小) wear down the hard and large over time.
