花 flower appears in some of the most enduringly beautiful lines of Chinese poetry. Tang poet 崔护 wrote: "去年今日此门中，人面桃花相映红" — "This very day last year, in this very gate, her face and the peach blossoms reflected each other red." The evanescence of the flower is the emblem of life's fragile beauty.
