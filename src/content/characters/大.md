大 is a pictogram of a man with arms spread — hence "great", "big". It is extraordinarily generative in compounds: 大学 dàxué the Great Learning / university, 大家 dàjiā everyone / a master of a field, 大道 dàdào the Great Way. Standing as a figure of scale, it pairs with 小 (xiǎo small, minor) to form one of Chinese thought's ubiquitous binary contrasts.
