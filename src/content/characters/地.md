地 is earth, soil, and, more broadly, the receptive, nurturing, bearer-of-all-things principle paired with the active, generative 天 heaven. The 坤 hexagram of the 易经 represents 地: "The superior person, with his vast virtue, bears all things" (君子以厚德载物). Together 天地 are the foundational polarity of Chinese cosmology.
