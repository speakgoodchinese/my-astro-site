言 means speech, a word, or a maxim. The ancient Confucian injunction 言行一致 (yán xíng yīzhì) — words and deeds must match — grounds sincerity (信 xìn) in a correspondence between what one says and what one does. 言 also functions as the radical 讠 in characters involving speech, writing, and discourse.
