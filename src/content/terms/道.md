### Meaning and Distinction

The Dao is the inherent pattern and trajectory governing all movement and existence. It is the objective reality, the underlying logic of how things unfold, hold together, and dissolve.

Unlike the anthropomorphic deity who acts as an external boss, judge, or creator standing above the universe and handing down moral laws, the Dao has no ego, no will, and no personal agenda. It is entirely immanent and non-intentional. It does not command the universe; it is the unforced grammar of how the universe operates. To align with it is not to seek divine favour or obey external commandments, but to move in harmony with the actual, unyielding conditions of reality rather than fighting against them.
