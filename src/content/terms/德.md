### Philosophical Meaning

In early Chinese philosophy, **De** represents the active manifestation or embodiment of the **Dao** in a specific being or thing. While Dao is the universal source, De is how that principle is expressed concretely. In Daoism, supreme virtue is unforced and flows naturally through the practice of **Wuwei**.
