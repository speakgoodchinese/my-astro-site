### Philosophical Meaning

**Wuwei** does not mean doing nothing; rather, it means avoiding unnatural, forced, or over-calculated actions. By acting in accordance with the natural tendencies of things and the **Dao**, one achieves goals smoothly without resistance or strife. It is the hallmark of the sage's leadership and personal conduct.
