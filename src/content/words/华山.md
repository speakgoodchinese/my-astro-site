华山 is Mount Hua, one of the 五岳 Five Great Mountains of traditional China. 山 (mountain) is the categorical noun; 华 (Huà) gives its specific name and location. The template [Proper Noun] + 山 to denote a named mountain is ubiquitous in Chinese toponymy and a textbook 尾重 structure.
