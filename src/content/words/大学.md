大学 is both the ancient Confucian text 大学 The Great Learning (the first of the Four Books in the Zhu Xi arrangement) and the modern word for university. In both cases, 学 (learning, school) is the categorical noun; 大 either means 'great' (the classical reading) or post-secondary level (the modern reading).
