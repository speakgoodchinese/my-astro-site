山水 literally mountains-and-water, and is the standard term for the classical Chinese landscape-painting genre that flourished from the Six Dynasties through Tang and Song. The solid vertical of 山 (mountains, enduring, stable) dominates the composition; 水 (water, flowing, yielding) fills the valleys, reflects the peaks, and animates the scene.
