中国 is the standard name for China in modern Mandarin. The compound is ancient, first appearing in the early Zhou bronze inscriptions to refer to the central polity that stood at the cultural centre of the world, as distinct from the peripheral 四方 four quarters. 中 (central, middle) carries the semantic weight; 国 (polity, state) supplies the categorical noun.
