国家 is the standard word for the state, the nation. Its two characters articulate the ancient political cosmology of 家国同构 the isomorphism of family and state: the smaller unit 家 (family, clan; the right-hand semantic core, the basic unit of human life) is nested within the larger unit 国 (polity, territory), and the two are morally homologous. Classified as 尾重 because 家 names the prototype, the categorical anchor; 国 specifies the scale.
