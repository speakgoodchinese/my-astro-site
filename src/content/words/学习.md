学习 is the modern everyday word for 'to study, to learn'. Its two characters reprise a famous couplet from the 论语 opening: "学而时习之，不亦说乎？" — "To learn (学) and repeatedly to put into practice what you have learned (习), is this not a pleasure?" 学 leads — it is the acquisition phase — and 习 qualifies it as disciplined repetition.
