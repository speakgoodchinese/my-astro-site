雪花 is a snowflake — poetically, a 'snow flower'. 花 (flower, petal-like form) is the noun category describing the delicate shape; 雪 (snow) is the material. The great Tang poet 李白 Li Bai wrote 燕山雪花大如席 "Over the Yan Mountains, snowflakes are as big as mats" — a hyperbole still recited to children learning poetry.
