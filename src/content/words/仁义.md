仁义 pairs 仁 humaneness (the cardinal Confucian virtue of care for others) with 义 justice/rectitude (the disposition to act rightly). As a compound it is a near-synonym for Confucian ethical goodness in its full shape; the first character 仁 anchors it as a virtue of care, with 义 specifying its outward, public side.
