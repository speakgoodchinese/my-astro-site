大道 is the Great Way. The character 道 is the semantic and categorical anchor; 大 merely amplifies it, specifying a Way of the highest order. The opening of 礼运 Lǐyùn's famous passage — 大道之行也天下为公 "When the Great Way prevails, all-under-heaven belongs to all" — uses it to mean the fundamental political-moral order of an ideal age. This is a paradigmatic 尾重 modifier-noun: the last character is the noun that is the meaning.
