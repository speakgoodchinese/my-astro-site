风景 is 'scenery, landscape view'. 风 (wind) names the invisible atmospheric quality — the feel, the light, the air of a place — that gives a place its characteristic 景 (sight, scene). The first character 风 carries the mood; the second character is the category: 'scenery' is a scene-as-inflected-by-its-atmosphere.
