人心 is the human heart-mind; often it means the collective feeling of the populace — the 'heart of the people'. 心 heart-mind is the noun head; 人 (human, of people) specifies whose heart. The proverb 人心向背 the people's inclinations (for or against you) names the most crucial asset in traditional statecraft.
