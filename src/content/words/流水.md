流水 is flowing water. 水 is the noun category; 流 flowing is the present-participle-like modifier describing the manner of water's movement. The flowing-water image dominates classical Chinese aesthetics: natural, unforced, shape-shifting to fit its container. 行云流水 'floating clouds and flowing water' names the effortless, natural style of great calligraphy, music, or prose. The semantic centre is 水; categorically this is a kind of water, not a kind of flow.
