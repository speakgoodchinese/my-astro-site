名言 is a celebrated saying or maxim. 言 — saying, speech, maxim — is the noun head; 名 (famous, renowned) is the attributive modifier that grades it. The entire compound therefore belongs to the 尾重 pattern: meaning is carried on the right, and the left character only narrows the category.
