道德 denotes morality — the concrete form that the Way (道) takes when embodied as personal or social virtue (德). In modern Mandarin it is the default term for 'morality, ethics'. The title of the classic 道德经 Dàodéjīng traditionally attributed to 老子 means "The Classic of the Way and Its Virtue". The 道 component dominates the compound's semantic centre — it names the source; 德 qualifies it as its manifestation.
