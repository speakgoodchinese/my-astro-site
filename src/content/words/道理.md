道理 is reason, principle, the 'why' of a matter. The leading character 道 gives the overarching direction or pattern, and 理 adds the specific ordering grain of a thing. The compound's meaning leans entirely on its first character: a 道理 is always a subspecies of 道, the articulable pattern of how something works.
