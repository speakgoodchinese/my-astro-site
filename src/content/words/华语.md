华语 Huáyǔ — 'the Chinese-language' from 华 Huá, the cultural-historical name for Chinese civilisation. This label is particularly common in Southeast Asian overseas contexts, where 'Chinese' can connote nationality rather than language; as with 汉语, the right-hand 语 is the semantic and grammatical head.
