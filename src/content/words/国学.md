国学 is National Learning, the modern revivalist name for the traditional Chinese scholarly corpus — classics, history, philosophy, belles-lettres — as a distinct pedagogical field. 学 is the categorical noun: a branch of learning. 国 is a modifier narrowing its scope to the national tradition.
