圣人 is the sage — the highest type of human being in the Chinese philosophical traditions. 人 is the categorical noun: a person; 圣 sage-hood, the modifier. To be a 圣人 is to be a 人 of the 圣 kind. The 道德经 famously says the sage has no fixed heart (mind) of his own; he takes the hearts of the hundred surnames (百姓) as his own.
