天地 heaven-and-earth is the native Chinese name for the cosmos as a whole, the ordered totality within which all human life takes place. 天 is the active generative principle that presides and moves; 地 is the receptive, nurturing bearer of things that completes them. All other pairings (阴阳/刚柔/男女) are mirrors of this master polarity, whose 天 is the semantic and cosmological leader.
