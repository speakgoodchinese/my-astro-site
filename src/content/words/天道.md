天道 is the Way of Heaven — the order that governs the natural and moral cosmos. 道 is the root noun; 天 qualifies it as the Way issuing from above. The 道德经 line 天道无亲 常与善人 — "The Way of Heaven has no favourites; it always sides with the good person" — captures this characteristically Chinese vision of a moral order without a personal judge.
